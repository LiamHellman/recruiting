Senior Backend Engineer, Payments Ledger — Larkspur Financial (fictional)

Larkspur builds the ledger that reconciles card payments for 4,000 small businesses across Canada. You'll join a team of six engineers who own the ledger service end to end.

What you'll do
- Design and ship features in our Django monolith and the services around it.
- Own data models and migrations in PostgreSQL, including partitioning for our largest tables.
- Build and operate asynchronous jobs with Celery and Redis.
- Review code, mentor two intermediate engineers, and take part in an on-call rotation.

What we're looking for
- 6+ years of professional software development.
- Expert Python and deep experience with Django.
- Strong SQL and PostgreSQL performance tuning.
- Experience with Celery or a similar task queue.
- Solid habits around automated testing (we use pytest).
- Excellent written communication.

Bonus points
- Kubernetes in production.
- Terraform.
- Experience on AWS.

We offer four weeks of vacation, a learning budget and a hybrid schedule from our Montréal office.
